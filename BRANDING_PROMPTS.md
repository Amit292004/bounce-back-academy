## 🛠️ 7. Parameter Cheat Sheet & Negative Prompts

### For Midjourney (`/imagine`):
* **Aspect Ratios:**
  * YouTube / Hero Banners: `--ar 16:9`
  * Instagram Posts: `--ar 4:5` or `--ar 1:1`
  * Mobile Wallpapers / Stories / Reels: `--ar 9:16`
* **Style & Version Flags:** `--v 6.1 --style raw --q 2`
* **Negative Prompt (`--no`):**
  ```text
  --no cartoon, ugly, deformed hands, extra fingers, blurry, low resolution, watermark, amateur drawing, oversaturated cartoonish colors, bad anatomy
  ```

### For Runway Gen-3 Alpha & Kling AI:
* **Camera Movement Tags to Add:**
  * `Cinematic slow push in, subtle handheld micro-movement`
  * `Fast orbit around subject, 360 degree sweep`
  * `Low angle heroic camera tilt up`
* **Motion Guidance:** Set motion scale between `4` and `6` for clean, realistic human expressions without morphing artifacts.
